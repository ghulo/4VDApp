What it does

Everything a shop owner checks, in one place

No spreadsheets, no notebooks behind the counter. Your team records sales on their phones and you see the whole picture from anywhere.

Live

Sales and revenue

Every sale and return, recorded at the counter from a phone. Daily, weekly and monthly totals without adding anything up.

Live

Stock and reorder levels

Know exactly what is on the shelf. Get alerted before a product runs out, not after a customer asks for it.

Profit, not just sales
